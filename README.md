# 🌱 SmartCrop — The Farm Time Machine

> **Explore your farm's future before you plant it.**

SmartCrop is a NASA-powered agricultural decision-support prototype that combines **NASA Earth observation data**, **low-cost local IoT measurements**, and **crop information** to help farmers explore feasible crop-rotation strategies under changing environmental conditions.

The project is being developed for the **NASA International Space Apps Challenge 2026**.

---

## 🚀 The Idea

Farmers cannot control rainfall, temperature, water availability, or changing environmental conditions.

But they can make decisions about what to plant next.

SmartCrop allows a farmer to:

1. Observe current farm conditions.
2. Combine local sensor measurements with NASA environmental data.
3. Select farming priorities.
4. Explore possible crop-rotation strategies.
5. Change environmental conditions using a **"What If?"** simulator.
6. Compare how different strategies respond to those conditions.

### The central question

> **"What could happen to my farm if conditions change—and what options do I have?"**

---

## 🛰️ Why NASA?

NASA provides environmental observations that can help describe conditions beyond what a single farm sensor can measure.

SmartCrop is planned to use NASA data for:

- Temperature and climate conditions
- Precipitation
- Humidity
- Regional soil moisture
- Vegetation/environmental indicators

NASA data is therefore part of the project's decision-making pipeline rather than simply being displayed on a map.

---

## 🔌 Local IoT

NASA provides regional environmental context, while local sensors provide observations from the farm.

### Planned hardware

- ESP32-S3
- DHT22 temperature/humidity sensor
- Soil-moisture sensor
- Optional EC/TDS measurement
